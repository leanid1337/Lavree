Size guide

Measure once, we cut to it

How to measure

Use a soft tape, over light clothing, and keep it level rather than tight. If you land between two sizes, tell us both — the waist is adjustable on most pieces and we can cut to the middle.

  • Waist — the narrowest part, usually just above the navel.
  • Hips — the widest part, standing with your feet together.
  • Height — barefoot, against a wall, so we can set the length.

About the fit

The silhouettes are wide on purpose. Kalhoty and šortky sit high with a drawstring waist, so they forgive a centimetre or two in either direction; tops are cut close through the shoulder and loose below it.
