A small studio in Prague, sewing clothes that move like air.

Handmade in Czechia

LAVRÉE studio began with one sewing machine and a stubborn idea: clothes should be made for a person, not for a warehouse. Every pair of kalhoty, every top and every dress is cut and sewn by hand in our Prague atelier — ručně vyrobeno v Čechách.

We work in small runs and to preorder only. Nothing is overproduced; everything is made because someone asked for it.
